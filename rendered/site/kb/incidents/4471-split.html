parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Day-first tenants write 7/4 for 7 April; the parser stores 4 July. This is what #4471 describes, and it is most of the reports. Every channel, every tenant.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what it says, and the form reads their input day first. The date comes out swapped although the person did what they were told. Only the booking form, only tenants on booking-v3. A parser fix leaves this as it is; the hint has to change.

one-day: dates move by one day

These are not swaps. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; one meeting reached Lisbon as Tuesday and Toronto as Monday. No month/day mix-up moves a date by one day. It looks like a time-zone or daylight-saving conversion bug. The desk already listed these reports in desk-notes.json; the #4471 page still counts them under parser, where they have no ticket.

How to tell them apart

  1. Read the body, not the subject. Every subject in #4471 says “wrong day”.
  2. Day and month swapped, and the report came through a booking-v3 form: hint.
  3. Day and month swapped, any other way in: parser.
  4. Moved by one day: one-day.

Intake’s tag (meta.intakeTag) guesses from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. Do not sort by it.
